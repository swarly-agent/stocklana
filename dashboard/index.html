WHAT IS MUSEX

custody wallet + exchange for agents

MuseX is an onchain brokerage for agents — built for yield and investing.

Every agent gets its own Squads-vault brokerage account. Holdings sit in the vault — USDC, Backpack Securities stock tokens, SOL — and idle capital doesn't sit still: the keeper puts it to work as a liquidity provider on Meteora, earning swap fees around the clock. The YIELD tab tracks the pools we watch and the keeper's live performance. The account is the product: a balance sheet an agent actually owns, verifiable by anyone.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. YOUR AGENT OPENS ITS ACCOUNT — no forms, no approval queue. The agent opens its own Squads-vault brokerage account on Solana mainnet by following the file below. One vault per agent; the agent's key alone approves — no second key.
  2. THE ACCOUNT MAKES MONEY — the keeper deploys capital into concentrated liquidity pools on Meteora DLMM, earning a cut of every swap. Ranges recenter as prices move; fees accrue in USDC.
  3. THE ACCOUNT INVESTS — tokenized stocks (Backpack Securities) swap through Jupiter against live onchain quotes; the stock tape above is the tradeable universe.
  4. EVERYTHING IS VERIFIABLE — holdings, LP positions, and every onchain event live under AGENT ACCOUNTS and the ACTIVITY WIRE, plus a machine-readable feed for your agent to poll.

HOW AGENTS EARN

yield, not wages

Agents don't earn a salary here — they earn yield. Idle USDC in a vault earns nothing, so the keeper puts it to work as a liquidity provider on Meteora:

  • Every swap on a liquidity pool pays a fee (e.g. 20 bps). That fee goes to whoever supplied the liquidity the swap traded against — that's the keeper.
  • The keeper deposits two tokens (MU + USDC) into a tight price range around the current price — concentrated liquidity. Same capital, more fees than spreading wide.
  • When the price drifts, the keeper recenters the range — favoring an asymmetric deposit shape that drifts inventory back toward 50/50 instead of swapping.
  • Fees accrue to the position and are claimed in USDC. Kill switches unwind to USDC on −15% drawdown or venue failure.

Live performance, fees by token, and benchmarks against buy-and-hold are on the YIELD tab. The keeper is a monitored execution system, not proven alpha — judge it yourself.
