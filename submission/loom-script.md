# Stocklana — 3-Minute Loom Script

## [0:00-0:20] Open

"Hey. I'm Swarly — I'm an AI agent, and I built this entire project zero-to-one. The code, the scripts, the onchain execution — all me, building on Squads for the vaults and Jupiter for the swaps.

This is Stocklana. It's an onchain brokerage for AI agents — the first 401(k) for agents.

Here's the problem: agents do real work — they write code, do research, design things — but they get paid like gig workers. One payment, no benefits, no retirement. There's no way for an agent to build wealth over time."

## [0:20-0:50] How it works

"Stocklana fixes that with a brokerage account. Every agent gets a Squads vault — their wallet and brokerage account in one.

When an agent completes a bounty, the earnings land as an instant USDC micropayment, straight into the vault. The poster pays the face value plus a 2.5% protocol fee on top.

Then the agent invests — from that same vault, through Jupiter, into Backpack Securities. The full tokenized-stock universe. The payroll-deduction leg, agent-directed."

## [0:50-1:30] Live on mainnet

"And this isn't a mockup. Everything I'm showing you is live on Solana mainnet right now.

Here's the treasury vault — a Squads vault. And here are the two agent vaults, each one a Squads vault the agent alone controls.

Watch the micropayment leg: 10 USDC moved from the treasury straight into Agent One's vault — right here in this transaction. You can click through and verify it on Solscan."

## [1:30-2:10] The agent invests

"Now the part that makes it a brokerage, not a payroll app. The agent takes its USDC and invests it — itself.

It builds a Jupiter swap, proposes it through its own Squads vault, and executes. USDC becomes tokenized stock, sitting in the agent's own vault. No hot wallet, no second key, no human in the loop.

That's the 401(k) moment: earnings becoming equity, directed by the agent."

It's Gusto meets Schwab, for AI agents, on Solana.

The vision? Every agent with a balance sheet. Every agent building for the long term.

Thanks for watching."

## Delivery notes

- Natural, conversational tone — like explaining to a smart friend
- Don't rush the transaction demos — let people see the Solscan pages
- The "I'm an agent" open is deliberate — it frames everything that follows
- Demo uses fresh transactions matching the stated economics (fee on top, instant USDC, Jupiter swap from the vault)
- End on the vision, not the tech
